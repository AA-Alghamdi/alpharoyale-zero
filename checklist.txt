== DATA-AVAILABILITY FINDING (superseded by PR #10) ==
The repo's CSVs richly encode CORE STATS (now sourced in PR #8) but are SPARSE on
granular mechanic columns. Even the pure-APK v1 extract only has:
  ChargeRange: 3 cards (Prince, DarkPrince, BattleRam)
  DeathDamage: 3 cards (Golem, Golemite, IceGolemite)
  CrownTowerDamagePercent: 1 card (Miner)
  MinimumRange / SpecialRange / DeathSpawnCharacter / SpawnCharacter: 0 cards
So most mechanics (min-range, full death-damage roster, status effects, spawn
tables) are NOT in the data; they remain hand-curated in cards.py/evolutions.py/
game.py, which already implements many of them. True 1:1 mechanics fidelity needs
a fuller/newer APK extraction than what's in the repo.
